# Tamil-Nadu-Assembly-Election-Analysis-2021-vs-2026-
This project analyzes constituency-level Tamil Nadu Assembly election data from 2021 and 2026 to identify changes in voting patterns, party performance, regional trends, and constituency-level outcomes.

Tamil Nadu Assembly Election Analysis — 2021 vs 2026
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes constituency-level Tamil Nadu Assembly election data from 2021 and 2026 to identify changes in voting patterns, party performance, regional trends, and constituency-level outcomes.

The analysis combines Python, Pandas and Tableau to transform raw election data into an interactive visual analytics story.

The primary objective is to understand what changed between the two election years, where the changes occurred, and how these changes varied across regions and constituencies.

🎯 Business / Analytical Objectives
Compare overall voting patterns between 2021 and 2026.
Analyze votes cast across different regions and constituencies.
Compare party-wise constituencies contested and won.
Calculate and compare party success rates.
Identify constituency-level changes between the two election years.
Analyze the regional distribution of constituency-level changes.
Present the findings through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

📊 Tableau Dashboard

The project contains 5 Tableau stories covering different stages of the analysis:

Story 1 — Overall Election Overview

Provides a high-level comparison of the 2021 and 2026 election results, including votes cast, constituencies contested, constituencies won, and party performance.

Story 2 — Regional Voting Analysis

Examines changes in votes cast across different regions and identifies regional variations between the two election years.

Story 3 - Overall Election Results 

Overall election Results with the constituency name, winning party, winning candidate and total votes secured

Story 4 — Party Performance & Success Rate

Compares party-wise performance using constituencies contested, constituencies won, and success rate.

Success Rate = Constituencies Won / Constituencies Contested × 100

Story 5 — Constituency-Level Flips

Identifies constituencies where the winning party changed between 2021 and 2026 and examines the geographic distribution of these changes.

Within the analyzed dataset, 91 constituencies shifted from DMK or AIADMK in 2021 to TVK in 2026, consisting of:

65 DMK → TVK
26 AIADMK → TVK
Story 5 — Key Insights

Summarizes the major findings from the analysis through KPI cards, charts, and geographic visualizations.

🔍 Key Findings
The distribution of constituency wins changed considerably between the two election years.
Regional voting patterns varied, with some regions showing an increase while others recorded a decline in votes cast.
Party success rates changed significantly between 2021 and 2026.
The analysis identified 91 constituency-level changes from DMK/AIADMK in 2021 to TVK in 2026.
The geographic analysis showed different regional concentrations for the constituency-level changes.

Note: These findings describe patterns observed in the analyzed dataset and are intended for data-analysis and visualization purposes.
